# Xiamen Prep Plan — Aug 2026 → Sep 2027 (13 months)

Target: **Xiamen University** (or an equivalent Chinese-language program),
Autumn 2027 intake. Profile: absolute beginner, fast encoder, weak retainer,
ADHD, interest-driven polymath. The app (Zhōng) is the delivery vehicle; this
doc is the curriculum it serves.

## 1. Goals by arrival

- **Language:** HSK3 level (~600 words, ~80 grammar points) — solid enough
  for campus life, daily conversations, and a strong placement result.
- **Pronunciation:** tones produced accurately on practiced phrases; can
  read any pinyin.
- **Survival:** order food, shop/bargain, transport, campus admin, health —
  in Mandarin.
- **Confidence:** 10+ turn conversations rehearsed via in-app dialogues.

Program note: XMU's OEC (海外教育学院) and similar programs run
multi-level Chinese-language tracks; textbooks commonly include *HSK
Standard Course* or *汉语教程* families — **confirm the specific textbook
after admission and re-point the HSK tracks' ordering if needed** (the
track system makes this a data change, not a code change).

## 2. Daily cadence (ADHD-realistic)

- **10–25 min/day**, shaped by the app's daily plan (F-103): due reviews +
  N new words + 1 short drill.
- **2-minute minimum bar**: on low days, only reviews count. The streak
  survives on that.
- **1 deep dive/week**: a rabbit-hole trail (etymology, symbolism, history)
  — the fun that keeps the system alive.
- **1 dialogue/week** from Phase 4 onward; **1 synthesis story/week** from
  Phase 3 onward (reading it counts as practice).

Bursts are welcome (hyperfocus), but the SRS queue — not study length — is
the source of truth. The app never asks for willpower; it asks for presence.

## 5. Survival modules (vocabulary domains)

1. People & campus — greetings, self-intro, classroom, classmates
2. Food & ordering — menus, ordering, dietary needs, paying
3. Shopping & bargaining — markets, sizes, prices, 多少钱, bargaining
4. Transport & directions — bus/taxi/DiDi, 怎么走, locations
5. Phone & admin — SIM card, registration, forms, 填表
6. Dorm & living — room, keys, laundry, maintenance
7. Health & pharmacy — 药店, symptoms, doctor visit
8. Money & bank — opening accounts, ATM, WeChat/Alipay pay
9. Small talk & weather — conversations with roommates, teachers
10. Xiamen city — 厦门 spots, culture notes (standard Mandarin only)

## 6. Interest channels as vocabulary amplifiers

The channels (F-301) are not side quests — they are where the most durable
encoding happens. Expected split by mid-course: ~65% track words, ~35%
interest words, all consolidated in one FSRS graph. Channels:

- **Art & culture** — painting, calligraphy, ceramics, film
- **Symbolism & metaphysics** — hanzi as symbol systems, 道/气/阴阳
  concepts, folk symbolism — a natural fit for his cognition
- **History** — dynastic arcs, Silk Road, maritime Fujian (Xiamen's own
  history — doubles as arrival prep)
- **Politics & economics** — China-in-the-world digest (F-303), leveled
- **Modern life** — slang, internet culture, student life

## 7. Assessment & self-correction

- **Monthly checkpoint** (table above) — measured by in-app data, not
  vibes: retention %, words reviewed, streak, tone accuracy.
- **Retention rule:** if FSRS predicted retention < 80% for 2 consecutive
  weeks, the daily plan (F-103) auto-reduces new words. Trust the dial,
  don't fight it.
- **Placement honesty:** target HSK3, accept HSK2 placement as a win for a
  13-month beginner — the app continues working in China either way.

## 8. Guardrails (what NOT to do)

- No traditional characters, no Cantonese/HK content (fixed scope).
- No handwriting requirement before departure (optional stroke layer only).
- No memorizing grammar tables — concepts (F-203) instead.
- No "big study day" plans — micro-sessions compound; binge-crash cycles
  are the enemy.
- No waiting for motivation — the 2-minute bar is always available.
