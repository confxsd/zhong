Cloudflare Workers + D1 backend powering the zhong.rome.markets Chinese learning companion. It provides vocabulary management, FSRS spaced repetition review (ts-fsrs v5, replacing the Leitner boxes), bidirectional translation teaching, session tracking, structured curriculum tracks (HSK1 seeded from `src/data/hsk1.ts`), AI-generated track lessons, a daily adaptive study plan, streaks and retention stats, Google TTS audio, and per-IP rate limiting via a Hono API.
